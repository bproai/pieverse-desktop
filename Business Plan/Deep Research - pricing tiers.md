## Clean, Pure Pricing Model

### Free Trial
- **Duration:** 30 days (fully featured)  
- **Purpose:** Allow both individual developers and business users to experience the full capabilities of the tool risk-free.

### Personal Tier
- **Price:** $2.99 per month (or $28.70/year with 20% annual discount when paid in full)  
- **Key Features:**  
  - Full access to core AI coding assistance and advanced control features  
  - Unlimited (or very high limit) usage for individual developers  
  - Seamless integration with popular developer environments (e.g., VS Code)  
  - Regular product updates and standard email support

### Business Tier
- **Price:** $39.99 per user/month  
- **Key Features:**  
  - Everything included in the Personal Tier for each user  
  - Business-specific tools such as centralized billing and administrative controls  
  - Enhanced security and compliance options  
  - Priority support and dedicated account management

---

**Important Note:**  
To unlock the full functionality of our product, users must have their own ChatGPT or Claude account and/or obtain the necessary API key(s). This requirement ensures that our tool serves as an advanced interface with granular control, rather than a standalone alternative to those AI platforms.
